Kri’binn

A semi-mechanical spider that accompanies you as a seventh companion.

Gilbertus Agumanus is a gnome tinkerer accompanied by two semi-mechanical spiders. The black one is his bodyguard; the green and grey one, Kri’binn, is for sale. Buy her, and that is where it all begins.

Where can you find Gilbertus?

GameLocation
BG2EE and EETWaukeen’s Promenade.
IWDEEKuldahar.
PSTEEThe Lower Ward.

Bring plenty of gold and negotiate with a character who has high Charisma. Depending on your ability scores and the options you choose, you may be able to get a discount.

Note: in PSTEE, all prices are divided by 5.

Kri’binn’s statistics

Hit Dice10 HD.
Armor Class4.
Attacks2 attacks per round, each dealing 1D8 points of slashing damage. Her legs are nonmagical.
Regeneration1 hit point per turn.
Fire resistance50%.
VulnerabilitiesElectricity: 25%; magic: 25%.
ImmunitiesNonmagical weapons, poison, disease, level drain, death effects and mind-affecting effects.

Movement

Kri’binn can follow you everywhere without taking up one of the six party slots. Press K to make her follow the protagonist.

Rest mode

When Kri’binn drops below 3 hit points, she enters rest mode: her back turns red, she becomes inactive and untargetable, and the AI ignores her. She regenerates faster for six rounds, then automatically restarts with 50% of her maximum hit points.

Beware of electricity! Whenever she takes electrical damage, she must make a saving throw vs. death with a +2 bonus. If she fails, she is destroyed in an explosion that deals 10D6 points of fire damage within a 10-metre radius, with a saving throw for half damage. Gnome tinkerers’ inventions often end up causing their deaths…

The potion compartment

Kri’binn has a compartment into which you can pour the potions offered through her dialogue. Their effects are applied to the spider. Potions from the L’Interplan and Jan’s Alchemy mods are also supported.

After use, the compartment locks for half the potion’s normal duration. You cannot pour anything into it during that time. By timing your potions carefully, you can therefore combine the effects of several potions.

  • Healing potions of all kinds, Potions of Invisibility and Potions of Disenchantment do not lock the compartment.
  • Potions that are of no use to the spider are not offered: protection against poison, disease or mental attacks, Wisdom or Intelligence boosts, improvements to thief or mage skills, and so on.

Kro’tann

Immediately after buying Kri’binn (and only at that moment), if you still have at least 20,000 gold pieces available, you can ask to buy Kro’tann. You will need much more gold than that to convince Gilbertus, however, and once again, Charisma, Wisdom and Intelligence will help you bring the price down.

Kro’tann: statistics — spoiler

Kro’tann is a half-golem spider that Gilbertus keeps for his own protection.

Kro’tann’s statistics

Hit Dice13 HD.
Armor Class0.
Attacks3 attacks per round, each dealing 1D10 points of slashing damage. Her legs are magical +1 weapons.
Regeneration1 hit point every 6 rounds.
Fire resistance100%.
VulnerabilityMagic: 25%.
ImmunitiesNonmagical weapons and +1 magical weapons, poison, disease, level drain, death effects and mind-affecting effects.
MovementMovement +2; she moves faster than Kri’binn.
Special abilityTrue Sight, 3 times per day.

Rest and potions

She has the same rest mode and potion compartment as Kri’binn.

She has no problems with electricity : she does not share Kri’binn’s risk of exploding.

Installation

This mod is designed for BG2EE, EET, IWDEE and PSTEE.

  1. Extract the mod folder Derats_KriBinn and its installer Setup-Derats_KriBinn.exe into your game folder, alongside chitin.key.
  2. Run Setup-Derats_KriBinn.exe, choose your language and install the “Gilbertus and his spiders” component.

To uninstall the mod, run the same program again and choose the uninstall option.
